# Amour: Pose. Flash. Obsess 

Amour is a fun, interactive web based photobooth app. Strike a pose, trigger the flash, pick a frame, snap a picture, and download your photo instantly as a JPG all from your browser, no physical photobooth required.

🔗 **Live demo:** https://amour-azure.vercel.app/


---

## ✨ Features

- 📸 **Live camera capture** — click a picture directly from your webcam
- ⚡ **Flash effect** — adds a realistic photobooth flash when you snap a photo
- 🖼️ **Frame selection** — choose from multiple decorative frames before capturing
- 💾 **Download as JPG** — save your captured photo instantly to your device
- 🎨 **Clean, playful UI** — smooth and simple experience across the app

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
- Camera capture: Browser MediaDevices / getUserMedia API
- Image export: Canvas API 

---

## 🖱️ Usage

1. Open the app in your browser and allow camera access.
2. Select a frame from the available options.
3. Strike a pose and click the capture button.
4. Watch the flash effect trigger as your photo is taken.
5. Preview your photo and click **Download** to save it as a JPG.
